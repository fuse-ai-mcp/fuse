# Fuse

The `fuse` MCP server connects to the user's Fuse workspace for B2B prospecting and outreach: people and company search, list building, contact enrichment, buying-signal monitors, account research, and email, LinkedIn and calling campaigns.

- Searches, enrichment and research spend the user's Fuse credits. Check the balance and tell the user the expected cost before large jobs.
- Show a sample of search results before saving or enriching at scale.
- Campaigns stay as drafts. Only launch or send when the user explicitly asks.
- Confirm before deleting data, pushing to a CRM or sending any message.
- Use IDs returned by tools; never guess them.

Docs: https://docs.fuseai.com/mcp/connect
